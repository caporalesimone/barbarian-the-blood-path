=== moving and fighting
[joystick 2] or [w a s d]  move
[fire] or [space]  attack with the skill
you have chosen: hold it to keep on
striking.

skills: choose the one fire uses
 [h] slash       level 1, one blow
 [j] whirlwind   level 3, all around
 [k] battle cry  level 6, stuns them
 [l] smash       level 12, double dice
powers: they start at once
 [u] rage        level 9, for 6 s the
               damage you take halves
 [o] frenzy      level 15, for 5 s two
               blows per fire, faster
{a skill shows in the hud only when}
{your level allows it.}

[1]-[4]  drink a potion of the belt
[5]  read a portal scroll
[i]  inventory      [m]  floor map
[c]  character      [run/stop]  menu
=== fire on places
stand on a place and press [fire]:
 stairs down   the next floor
 stairs up     the floor above
               (from floor 1: village)
 crypt         floor 1
 portal        on a floor: the village
               in the village: back to
               the floor you left
 shop door     smith or alchemist
 inn door      your inn chest
 chest         open it, if no monster
               is near; a key chest
               needs a key.

walk on the fountain: it heals you.

{every floor remembers what you did}
{there when you leave it.}
=== inventory, chests and shops
every screen works the same way:
 [w] [s] or [stick] up/down: move
 [fire] select   [run/stop] back
inventory [i]   {the pack holds 8 items}
 [fire] use   [del] drop   ([i] closes)
 {a broken item (red) stays on you but}
 {does nothing: repair it or swap it.}
chest: [fire] takes an item
shops: smith, alchemist
 [fire] on wares: buy (your ¤, then
 the inn's). on the pack: sell. smith:
 [fire] on worn gear or [r]: repair.
 a potion: choose its belt slot.
inn chest (your inn): 24 items
 [fire] moves an item between pack
 and chest; on your ¤: into the chest
 in tens (0..9 stay on you).
 [right] from the pack to the chest.
 [fire] on a page number: next page.
 [fire] on an equipped slot: choose
 what to wear ([run/stop] cancels).
map [m]: [fire], [m] or [run/stop]
=== menus and saving
game menu [run/stop]
 resume, save game, quit game
 {quit game: back to the main menu}
 autosave: levels 5-6, 10-11, 15-16
{never switch off while saving!}
main menu: choose a barbarian to see
 his sheet: start, delete, back.
questions: [y] or [fire] = yes,
 [n] or [run/stop] = no.
 quit the game: back to basic
new barbarian
 name, [return], seed, [return]
 seed: 6 letters or digits, [f1] random
 then w/s choose, fire edits or starts
 [del] delete   [run/stop] back
 {the same seed makes the same dungeon.}

if you die you lose half of the ¤
you carry (the ¤ in your inn chest
is safe) and wake up in the village.
you can carry up to 10000¤: keep
the rest in your inn chest.

[4]        [5]       [6]   [7]          [8]

    and max (10000+ shows as 10k)

    broken. c3 l4: chapter 3, level 4

[6] u o: rage, frenzy. yellow: on,
    white: ready, cyan: recharging
[7] keys for the key chests
[8] belt: potions [1]-[4] (blue while
    they recharge), portal scroll [5]
