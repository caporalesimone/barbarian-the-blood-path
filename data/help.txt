=== all the keys
 <moving>
   [joystick 2] or [w a s d]
   [fire] or [space]	strike, talk

 <skills and powers>
   [h] [j] [k] [l]	choose a skill
   [u] [o]	rage, frenzy

 <belt>
   [1] [2] [3] [4]	drink a potion
   [5]	portal scroll

 <screens>
   [i]	inventory
   [m]	floor map
   [c]	your character
   [q]	your quests
   [run/stop]	game menu
=== moving and fighting
 <walk>
   [joystick 2] or [w a s d]

 <fight>
   [fire] or [space]	strike with the
   	chosen skill
   hold [fire]	keep striking

 <how fast you strike>
   your weapon sets the pace, and a
   low dex makes you slower: with
   dex 18 you strike as fast as the
   weapon allows.
   {raise dex with the points of the}
   {character screen [c].}

 the skills are in the next pages.
=== skills and powers
 a skill is the attack that [fire] uses.
 choose it with its key:
   [h] slash	level 1
   [j] whirlwind	level 3
   [k] battle cry	level 6
   [l] smash	level 12

 a power starts at once with its key:
   [u] rage	level 9
   [o] frenzy	level 15

 every skill but slash and both powers
 need time to recharge after use.

 {a skill shows in the hud only when}
 {your level allows it. from level 17}
 {a roll of 19 or 20 is a critical.}
=== skill: slash (h)
 your basic attack, from level 1.

 <what it does>
   one blow at the monster in front
   of you: one attack roll, the dice
   of your weapon plus your str.

 <recharge>
   the pace of your weapon, slower
   with a low dex.

 <tip>
   hold [fire]: it strikes again as
   soon as it can.
=== skill: whirlwind (j)
 from level 3.

 <what it does>
   you spin around: one attack roll
   for every monster close to you,
   on every side.

 <recharge>
   2 seconds.

 <tip>
   use it when they surround you:
   one [fire], many blows.
=== skill: battle cry (k)
 from level 6.

 <what it does>
   a cry that reaches the monsters
   around you, even a few steps away.
   each one makes a saving throw: if
   it fails, it is stunned for
   2 seconds and does not move or hit.

 <recharge>
   15 seconds.

 <tip>
   the higher your str, the harder
   the saving throw.
=== skill: smash (l)
 from level 12.

 <what it does>
   a heavy blow around you: two attack
   rolls (the best counts), double
   dice, and the monster hit is
   stunned for half a second, even
   a boss.

 <recharge>
   6 seconds.
=== power: rage (u)
 from level 9. it starts at once.

 <what it does>
   for 6 seconds the damage you take
   is halved.

 <recharge>
   30 seconds.

 <in the hud>
   {yellow: on   white: ready}
   {cyan: recharging}
=== power: frenzy (o)
 from level 15. it starts at once.

 <what it does>
   for 5 seconds every [fire] strikes
   twice (battle cry once), and every
   blow that hits makes you run
   faster: up to twice your speed.

 <recharge>
   30 seconds.

 <in the hud>
   {yellow: on   white: ready}
   {cyan: recharging}
=== fire on places
 stand on a place and press [fire]:

   stairs down	the next floor
   stairs up	the floor above
   	{floor 1: village}
   crypt	floor 1
   portal	on a floor: the
   	village; in the
   	village: back to
   	the floor you left
   shop door	smith or alchemist
   inn door	your inn chest
   chest	open it, if no
   	monster is near
   	{key chest: a key}

 walk on the fountain: it heals you.
 {every floor remembers what you did.}
=== people of the village
 <hilde>
   she waits in the fountain square.
   stand next to her and press [fire]:
   she gives you her quest.

 <villagers>
   they walk around the village. when
   you come close they stop, so you
   can talk to them.

 <the quest giver>
   now and then a villager has a quest
   for you: a yellow [!] over his head
   and quest giver at the top of the
   screen. stand next to him, [fire].

 {the [!] goes away once you take}
 {the quest.}
=== your quests (q)
 [q] shows the quests you took.

 <the list>
   [w] [s] or [stick]	choose
   [fire]	open the quest
   [run/stop] or [q]	back

 <a quest>
   title, chapter, its status (in
   yellow, at the top), the story
   and the reward.
   [w] [s] or [stick]	scroll the text
   [run/stop] or [fire]	back to the list

 {up and more on the box: there is}
 {more text above or below.}
=== your character (c)
 every level from 2 to 20 gives you
 1 stat point to spend.

   str	attack, damage,
   	battle cry
   dex	armor class,
   	attack speed
   con	hit points

 <the keys>
   [w] [s]	choose
   [a] [d] or [-] [+]	a point less, more
   [fire] on a stat	a point more
   [fire] on confirm	spend the points
   [fire] on undo	take them back
   [run/stop] or [c]	back

 {nothing is spent until you confirm.}
 {yellow arrow in the hud: points to}
 {spend.}
=== respec
 a respec token gives back all the
 points you spent: you can choose
 again.

 <where to find them>
   killing the final boss.

 <how to use one>
   [c], then respec (it shows only
   if you have a token), [y] or
   [fire] to confirm.

 {the screen shows how many tokens}
 {you have.}
=== inventory (i)
 every screen works the same way:
   [w] [s] or [stick]	move
   [fire]	select
   [run/stop]	back

 <inventory>
   [fire]	use or wear
   [del]	drop
   [i]	close
   {the pack holds 8 items.}

 {a broken item (red) stays on you}
 {but does nothing: repair it or}
 {swap it.}

 <floor map>
   [fire] [m] [run/stop]	close
=== chests and shops
 <chest on a floor>
   [fire]	take an item

 <smith, alchemist>
   [fire] on wares	buy (your ¤,
   	then the inn's ¤)
   [fire] on the pack	sell
   [fire] on worn gear	repair (smith)
   [r]	repair (smith)
   {a potion: choose its belt slot.}

 <inn chest> {(every inn): 24 items}
   [fire]	between pack and
   	chest
   [fire] on your ¤	into the chest in
   	tens (0-9 stay on)
   [right]	from pack to chest
   [fire] on a page	next page
   [fire] on a slot	what to wear
   	([run/stop] cancels)
=== menus and saving
 <game menu> [run/stop]
   resume, save game, quit game
   {quit game: back to the main menu}

 <saving>
   save game in the menu, or by itself
   when you travel to a new chapter
   {never switch off while saving!}

 <questions>
   [y] or [fire]	yes
   [n] or [run/stop]	no

 <main menu>
   choose a barbarian to see his
   sheet: start, delete, back.
   quit the game: back to basic.
=== new barbarian, death
 <new barbarian>
   name, [return], seed, [return]
   seed: 6 letters or digits
   [f1]	random seed
   [w] [s]	choose
   [fire]	edit or start
   [del]	delete
   [run/stop]	back
   {the same seed makes the same}
   {dungeon.}

 <death>
   you lose half of the ¤ you carry
   (the ¤ in your inn chest is safe)
   and wake up in the village.

 <the ¤ you carry>
   up to 10000¤: keep the rest in
   your inn chest.
